## 1. Unit Economics & Key Assumptions

### Macro Drivers
- **Gross Merchandise Value (GMV):** Total dollar value of machine-to-machine settlements routed through HAICHAIN.
- **Protocol Take-Rate:** 0.05% (5 basis points) on every sub-cent transaction.
- **Enterprise Subscriptions:** $999/month average across enterprise AI swarms requiring dedicated validator priority lanes.
